Contact

This shop is run by Justin, the person behind @itsjustinjapan, from Australia.

Email hello@itsjustinjapan.com for anything about an order. You can also reply to your order email. I usually answer within two business days.

Refunds

The guide and planners are digital products you get straight away, so I don't offer refunds if you simply change your mind.

If something's wrong, I'll make it right. That includes:

  • the download or a planner link doesn't work
  • you were charged twice
  • the product isn't what's described on this page

Email me with your order email address and I'll send a working link, fix the problem, or refund you.

Privacy

This section explains what personal information itsjustinjapan collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Questions can be sent to hello@itsjustinjapan.com.

Information we collect

  • Orders: your name, email address, the products purchased, the amount paid, any discount code used, and whether you opted in to marketing emails.
  • Reviews: if you submit a review, your rating, review text, display name and email address.

How we use it

  • To deliver your purchase and provide customer support.
  • To keep business and tax records.
  • To confirm that reviews come from genuine customers. Your email address is never published.
  • If you opted in, to send occasional emails about Japan travel and new guides. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details are sent directly to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the shop: Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (website hosting, file storage and spam protection) and Resend (order emails). They process your information only to provide these services on our behalf.

Reviews

Reviews are only published with your permission and after they have been read. Only your display name, rating and review are shown. You can ask for your review to be changed or removed at any time.

Retention

Order records are kept for as long as required for tax purposes (currently five years in Australia). Other information is kept only while it is needed, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

We do not sell or rent your personal information. You can ask to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing hello@itsjustinjapan.com, subject to any records we must keep by law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
