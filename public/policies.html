Contact

This shop is run by Justin, the person behind @itsjustinjapan, from Australia.

Email hello@itsjustinjapan.com for anything about an order. You can also reply to your order email. I usually answer within two business days.

Refunds

The guide and planners are digital products you get straight away, so I don't offer refunds if you simply change your mind.

If something's wrong, I'll make it right. That includes:

  • the download or a planner link doesn't work
  • you were charged twice
  • the product isn't what's described on this page

Email me with your order email address and I'll send a working link, fix the problem, or refund you. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, or the consumer laws where you live.

Privacy

What I collect when you buy: your name, email address, what you bought, the amount paid, any discount code, and whether you ticked the box to get emails from me.

Payments: are handled by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and I never see or store them.

How it's used: to deliver your order, help if something goes wrong, and keep records for tax. If you ticked the box, I may also send you Japan tips and news about new guides. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Visit statistics: to see which posts bring people here, the shop counts visits by where they came from (for example TikTok or Instagram). Your browser stores a random ID for 30 days so a later purchase counts for the right source. It isn't linked to your name, and no advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used.

Reviews: if you leave a review, I keep your rating, review, name and email. Your email is only used to check you bought the guide and is never shown. Your review and name only appear on this site if you agree to it, and I read every review before it goes up. Email me to have yours changed or removed.

Who else handles your data: Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (hosting, storage and a bot check on checkout), and Resend (sending your order email). They process it only to provide those services.

Your choices: I don't sell your information. Email me to see, correct or delete what I hold about you, unless I have to keep it for tax records.
