Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how itsjustinjapan (“we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses and otherwise handles personal information in connection with the website at shop.itsjustinjapan.com (the “Site”) and the digital products sold through it (the “Products”). We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles to the extent they apply to us. By using the Site or purchasing a Product, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Personal information we collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us directly, including:

  • (a) Order information: your name, email address, the Products purchased, the amount paid, any promotional code applied, and your marketing preferences;
  • (b) Review information: where you submit a review, your rating, review content, chosen display name and email address; and
  • (c) Correspondence: the content of any communications you send to us.

We do not collect or store payment card details. Payments are processed by our payment provider as described in section 5.

3. Purposes of collection and use

We collect, hold and use personal information for the following purposes:

  • (a) to process your order and deliver the Products to you;
  • (b) to provide customer support and respond to enquiries;
  • (c) to verify that reviews are submitted by genuine customers;
  • (d) to detect and prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised use of the Site;
  • (e) to maintain business records and comply with our legal, accounting and taxation obligations; and
  • (f) where you have consented, to send you marketing communications in accordance with section 6.

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following lawful bases: performance of a contract (purposes (a) and (b)); our legitimate interests (purposes (c) and (d)); compliance with a legal obligation (purpose (e)); and your consent (purpose (f)).

4. Reviews

Reviews are published only where you have expressly consented to publication and following our review. Only your display name, rating and review content are published; your email address is never published. You may request that your review be amended or removed at any time.

5. Disclosure to third parties

We disclose personal information only to the extent necessary to operate the Site and supply the Products, to the following service providers, who process it on our behalf and subject to their own privacy and security obligations:

  • (a) Stripe, Inc. and its affiliates (payment processing);
  • (b) Cloudflare, Inc. (website hosting, data storage and security services); and
  • (c) Resend (transactional email delivery).

We may also disclose personal information where required or authorised by law. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

6. Marketing communications

We will send you marketing communications only if you have opted in. Each communication will include a functional means of unsubscribing, and you may withdraw your consent at any time in accordance with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth).

7. Overseas disclosure

Our service providers may store or process personal information outside Australia, including in the United States and other jurisdictions in which they operate. By providing your personal information, you acknowledge that it may be transferred to and handled in those jurisdictions. We take reasonable steps to ensure that such providers handle personal information in a manner consistent with this Privacy Policy.

8. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure, including the use of encrypted connections and reputable service providers. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Retention

We retain order records for the period required by applicable taxation law (currently five years in Australia). Other personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes set out in section 3, after which it is deleted or de-identified.

10. Access, correction and deletion

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at hello@itsjustinjapan.com. We will respond within a reasonable period, subject to any exceptions or record-keeping obligations under applicable law. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing and to data portability.

11. Children

The Site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

12. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us at hello@itsjustinjapan.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) or, where applicable, your local data protection authority.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may amend this Privacy Policy from time to time. The amended version takes effect when published on the Site, and the date at the top of this page indicates when it was last updated.
