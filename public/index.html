Questions

Why not just ask ChatGPT or another AI?

You can, and it's handy for quick questions on the go. But AI has never been to Japan. It's read about it, so what you get is the average trip: the same famous spots everyone else is queuing for, with no real sense of what's actually worth your time.

It also misses the little things that make a trip. Which konbini snacks are actually great, and fun to try without being too out there. How to order your days so you see the most without being exhausted by day four. You only learn those by being there, and I've been more than ten times.

Everything here is hand-picked by me and kept up to date. You get a finished, day-by-day plan you can follow straight away, not an evening of prompts and double-checking.

That's who it's written for. It starts with the basics (entry, eSIMs, IC cards, cash) and the planner gives you a full route to follow. If you've been before, the city pages and food picks are still worth it.
